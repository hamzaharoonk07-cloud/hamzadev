Who I am

about()

I'm a full stack developer and graphic designer based in Karachi. On the front end I work in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, jQuery and React. Behind it I write PHP against MySQL, and Node, Express and MongoDB where the project calls for it — logins, CRUD, admin panels, and the database design that holds them together.

I came at this from design first. Logos, brand identities and digital assets taught me composition, colour and type before I ever wrote a layout — and that's still how I build. The interface gets designed properly, then coded, then wired to real data.

All five projects here are live, from an organic storefront on its own domain to a MERN career-guidance platform. Among them is CARE Group, a deployed PHP and MySQL doctor-appointment portal with separate patient, doctor and administrator panels — role-based logins, a live availability grid, and the schema underneath it.

I'm studying for my Advanced Diploma in Software Engineering at Aptech alongside my Intermediate, and going deeper into advanced JavaScript patterns, relational database design, and modern design systems.

PathSeeker — Career Passport

Ten honest questions, matched against 38 real careers. Every result shows its reasoning and its trade-offs, then lays out a six-stage route into the job. Staged as a boarding-pass journey, on a MERN stack with a hand-written CSS design system — no UI or animation library.

live·healthcare · full stack care-pk.infinityfreeapp.com

CARE Group — Appointment Portal

A three-role portal built on PHP and MySQL. Patients search specialists by city and book from a doctor's live availability grid; doctors run their sittings, leave and appointment book; an admin maintains doctors, cities, logins and the public site's own content. Localised for Pakistan — ten cities, fees in rupees, Rescue 1122 — with a Perl script for the management report.
